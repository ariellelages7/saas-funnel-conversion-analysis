Key insights & prioritized recommendations

Priority 1 — Checkout is bleeding 92% of users (worth $151K/year to fix)

The checkout stage converts only 8% against an industry benchmark of 75%. Root causes typically include unexpected costs, mandatory account creation, or a long form. Recommendation: guest checkout, show total cost earlier, reduce form fields to minimum.

Priority 2 — Cart abandonment at 70% signals trust or pricing friction

Only 30% of users who add to cart proceed to checkout — well below the 65% benchmark. Recommendation: add social proof at cart, clear returns policy, A/B test a cart summary with savings highlighted.

Priority 3 — Overall conversion (0.14%) is 10–20x below SaaS benchmark

A healthy SaaS funnel converts 2–3% of visitors. Fixing checkout and cart alone could close most of this gap. Homepage and product page are above benchmark — the problem is concentrated in the bottom half of the funnel.

What's working — top of funnel is above benchmark

Homepage retains 100% to the next step (benchmark: 45%), and product page converts 50% to cart (benchmark: 35%). Top-of-funnel messaging and positioning are strong — investment should focus on fixing the bottom, not rebuilding the top.
